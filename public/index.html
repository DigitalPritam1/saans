How We Estimate This

Exposure Hours Avoided: Calculated by comparing your profile schedule against CPCB/Google Air Quality API diurnal inversion curves. Typical ground-level PM2.5 spikes by 200-350% between 5:00 AM and 9:30 AM due to planetary boundary layer compression. Moving heavy exertion outside this window directly averts acute particulate deposition in deep alveolar airways.

CO₂ Savings: Based on Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) average vehicular emissions (~110g CO₂/km for small 2-wheelers, ~160g CO₂/km for petrol cars) versus electrified mass transit (~15g CO₂/passenger-km on Delhi Metro).

Indoor Protection: Damp-towel seals and controlled kitchen cross-ventilation have been empirically shown in urban Indian low-cost housing studies to cut indoor infiltration of coarse PM10 and PM2.5 by 15-30% in unsealed apartments.
